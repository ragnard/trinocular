# Contributing

Trinocular is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE), but it is **not currently
accepting contributions**. Pull requests will be closed without review. This is a
single-maintainer project, and reviewing, merging and then owning outside changes is more than
it can take on for now. You are welcome to fork it under the terms of the license.

What does help:

- **Bugs and feature requests**: open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/ragnard/trinocular/issues).
  For a bug, include the version (at the foot of the account menu, or the image tag), what you
  did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
- **Security problems**: do not open a public issue. Report them privately, as described in
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
