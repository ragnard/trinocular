A second kind of document
Nothing new in the chrome. A document is whatever the middle column is showing; the header rail names it, ⌘P switches between them, and the connection chip still says which cluster it belongs to. Only the icon and the chip menu tell you which kind you are looking at.

Both are a header rail over two surfaces split by the same draggable divider, and the lower surface of each is an ordinary results table — so selection, the anchor strip and the inspector work identically on a snapshot list and on query rows, with no second implementation. The inspector sits outside the document column entirely, which is why it does not care which kind is open.
