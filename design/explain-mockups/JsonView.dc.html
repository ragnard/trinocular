Why keep it
It is the whole feature in an afternoon — the statement already runs, the response already comes back, and a read-only monaco already exists in the app. Ship this first, and every richer view becomes a rendering choice over data you already have on the client.
And keep it after
Any renderer will drop fields it does not understand, and Trino adds node kinds between releases. A raw tab means an unrecognised plan is still readable, and it is the thing people paste into an issue.
