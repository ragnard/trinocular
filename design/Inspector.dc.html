The inspector, beside the rows it came from
It is a region of the results pane, not a layer over it: the table keeps its rows on screen and the two share a draggable divider. Closed it costs nothing; open it takes as much room as the selection needs. A selection is a block — any number of rows deep, any number of columns wide — and every row in it becomes its own document.
